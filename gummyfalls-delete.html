How to request deletion

To delete your Gummy Falls account and all data stored for it on our servers:

  1. Email alimughal198777@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in to Gummy Falls.
  2. Subject: Delete my Gummy Falls account.
  3. Include your in-game nickname, and your player ID if you know it (it is shown on your profile screen).
  4. We will delete your account within 30 days of your request and reply to the same email address to confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

Your Berry Float server profile is deleted in full: your nickname, equipped cosmetic items, earned Cup medals, Weekly Berry Cup results and your player ID. After deletion, no account data is kept. Cup standings that were already published show only a nickname; once your account is deleted, the nickname is removed from future standings.

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion. There is no retention period beyond the up-to-30 days needed to process your request.

Data on your own device

Your game progress stored on your device (cleared batches, coins, settings and sound preferences) is not on our servers. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Note

The Gummy Falls online service is not hosted yet in this version, so no account data is currently stored on any server. If you signed in before the service went live, there is nothing to delete on our servers, and your request will be confirmed as such.

See also the Gummy Falls Privacy Policy.
