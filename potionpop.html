Privacy Policy — Potion Pop Color Sort

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · App: Potion Pop Color Sort (com.peaknova.potionpop) · Developer: PeakNova

Potion Pop Color Sort is a magical colour-sort puzzle game: pour colourful potions between glass test tubes until each tube holds a single colour. You can play it without creating an account.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Game progress — your current level, stars earned, coin balance, owned and equipped cosmetics (tube styles, pour trails, shop themes, kitten outfits), booster stock, and your music and sound settings.
  • No Ads ownership — whether you own the optional one-time No Ads purchase, so the game knows not to show ads.

This data lives only on your device. We operate no servers that receive your gameplay data, and the game has no account, no sign-in, and no leaderboard. Coins are earned by playing (completing tubes and levels, or choosing to watch a rewarded ad); there are no coin packs to buy with real money.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and the level-complete screen, an interstitial ad after every third win, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an extra booster or 100 coins. You never have to watch an ad to play.

Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase — a one-time No Ads membership — processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment card details. No Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase: once bought, it is yours forever and can be restored on a new device through Google Play.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising trackers of our own.
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no user accounts, and no way for players to share personal information with each other.
