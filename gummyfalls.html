Privacy Policy — Gummy Falls

Developer: PeakNova (Ali Shahzad)
Contact: alimughal198777@gmail.com
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Overview

Gummy Falls can be played without creating an account. Most of your game data never leaves your device. The only online features are the optional Weekly Berry Cup, Showcase and cloud save, described below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared batches, coins, owned cosmetics, settings and sound preferences) is stored only on your device.

Online features (optional)

Joining the Weekly Berry Cup, publishing a Showcase or enabling cloud save asks you to sign in with Google. Signing in sends a Google ID token to our Berry Float server so it can verify your identity; your public profile then carries only your nickname, equipped cosmetic items, earned medals and a stable player ID. No email address is ever sent in a public payload. The online service is not hosted yet in this version, so signing in cannot complete and no gameplay data is sent anywhere.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to earn 20 coins) and an interstitial ad after a batch win. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to serve ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The Soda Shop offers one optional purchase, the Rose Gold Float cosmetic set, through Google Play Billing. The product is not yet available for purchase; its price will be set in Google Play Console before it is offered.

Analytics & crash reporting

The game does not run third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Account deletion

You can delete your Gummy Falls account and all data stored for it on our servers at any time. See Delete your Gummy Falls account for the steps: email alimughal198777@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Gummy Falls account", and your server profile (nickname, equipped items, medals, Cup results and player ID) is deleted within 30 days, with nothing kept after that.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If a future version adds new online features, advertising formats or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
