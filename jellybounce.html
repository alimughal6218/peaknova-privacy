Privacy Policy — Jelly Bounce

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · App: Jelly Bounce (com.peaknova.jellybounce) · Developer: PeakNova

Jelly Bounce is a casual arcade game: slide a paddle to bounce a squishy jelly into fruit, build combos, and climb levels. You can play it without creating an account.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Game progress — your best score, coins, owned and selected jelly skins and paddle colours, Daily Challenge streak and best score, levels, sound setting, and whether you have seen the first-time tutorial.
  • Your No Ads purchase — whether you own the No Ads upgrade, so ads stay off after reinstalls (the purchase itself is verified with Google Play).

This data lives only on your device. We operate no servers that receive your gameplay data, and the game has no account, no sign-in, and no leaderboard.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an interstitial ad after every second game over, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to rescue a missed bounce). If you buy No Ads, interstitial ads stop; rewarded ads remain available only if you choose them.

Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. In the UK and EU, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase — No Ads ($2.99, one-time) — processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment card details. The purchase is tied to your Google Play account and can be restored on a new device with the in-game Restore button.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising trackers of our own.
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no user accounts, and no way for players to share personal information with each other.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your phone. Your No Ads purchase remains tied to your Google Play account and can be restored if you reinstall. To ask anything about your data, email alimughal198777@gmail.com.
