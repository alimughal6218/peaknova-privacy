Privacy Policy — Candy Boutique Sort

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · App: Candy Boutique Sort (com.peaknova.candysort) · Developer: PeakNova

Candy Boutique Sort is a cozy candy-sorting puzzle game: sort the candies into the right jars, earn coins and stars, and help the Candy Shop Bunny run her boutique. You can play it without creating an account.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Game progress — your current level, stars earned, coin balance, total wins, booster stock (Undo, Hint, Add Jar), your jar skins and bunny outfits (which you own and which is equipped), whether you own the No Ads purchase, and your sound and music settings.

This data lives only on your device. We operate no servers that receive your gameplay data, and the game has no account, no sign-in, and no leaderboard. Coins are earned by playing (completing tubes and winning levels) and spent in the in-game Shop on boosters, jar skins and bunny outfits. The only thing you can buy with real money is the optional one-time No Ads purchase (see below).

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (to double your coins after a win, for an extra Undo or Hint when you run out, or for a free Add Jar when you are stuck), and an interstitial ad after every third win. You never have to watch an ad to play. If you buy the No Ads purchase (below), the game shows no ads at all.

Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game offers exactly one purchase: No Ads — a one-time purchase of $4.99 that removes all ads from the game permanently. There is no other purchase: coins, boosters, jar skins and bunny outfits are all earned or bought with coins inside the game, never with real money. The No Ads purchase is processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details. If you reinstall the game or switch devices, you can restore the purchase from the Shop ("Restore purchase") with the same Google account.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising trackers of our own.
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no user accounts, and no way for players to share personal information with each other.
