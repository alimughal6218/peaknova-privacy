Privacy Policy — Tidy Titans

Effective: October 25, 2026 · Developer: PeakNova

Tidy Titans is a cozy sorting-puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

Information we collect

  • Account information (optional sign-in). You can play Tidy Titans fully as a guest without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name and email address through Firebase Authentication so your progress can follow you across devices.
  • Game data. Your coins, league tier, season scores, boosters, Titan Pass status, energy, and settings are stored — on your device, and in our cloud database (Google Firestore) if you are signed in.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases (Titan Pass, coin packs, energy refills) are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details; Google shares only a purchase confirmation with us.

What we do NOT collect

  • No location data.
  • No access to your camera, photos, or contacts.
  • No advertising identifiers — the app shows no third-party ads and does no ad tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs profiling your behavior.

How we use your information

  • To save and restore your game progress across devices.
  • To operate the global leaderboard and weekly league seasons.
  • To fulfill your in-app purchases.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data sharing

Your data is processed by the following service providers, each under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) — account and cloud-save hosting.
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing.

Data retention and deletion

Your game data is kept for as long as your account uses the app. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by emailing alimughal198777@gmail.com from the email address linked to your game account. We will delete your cloud-stored data within 30 days. Data stored only on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Children

Tidy Titans is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page, and the effective date will be revised accordingly.
