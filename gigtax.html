Privacy Policy — GigTax

Last updated: September 30, 2026 · App: GigTax — Gig Worker Tax App (com.peaknova.gigtax) · Developer: PeakNova

GigTax is a year-round tax autopilot for US gig workers and freelancers: a mileage logbook with automatic trip detection, receipt capture, deduction tracking, live tax estimates, and quarterly-payment tools.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Trips and mileage you record or that automatic trip detection logs (start/end locations, distance, timestamps, your labels and notes).
  • Receipts you photograph or import, including the amounts and merchant names extracted by on-device text recognition.
  • Income, deductions, goals, and settings you enter (Tax Tools tab, income goals, home-office figures, account preferences).

This data lives in a local database on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you sign in and enable cloud backup.

Cloud backup (only if you sign in)

If you create an account and sign in, the app backs up your tax data to Google Firebase services so it survives a lost or replaced phone:

  • Sign-in identity — your email address, via Firebase Authentication (Google sign-in or email).
  • Your tax records — trips, receipts, income, and deductions, synced to Cloud Firestore.
  • Receipt photos — uploaded to Firebase Storage.

You can delete your cloud data at any time by asking us at the contact email below; deleting your account removes the synced records from Firebase.

Location

  • While-you-use location is used to record trips when you press Start (manual tracking).
  • Background location is used only for the optional automatic trip detection: when it is switched ON, the app watches for driving in the background, records the trip automatically, and shows a persistent tracking notification. Turning the switch off stops all background location use immediately.

Location history is stored as part of your trip log and is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than your mileage logbook.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to capture or attach a receipt. Photos are used for the receipt record (with on-device text extraction) and backed up to Firebase Storage only if you have cloud backup enabled.

Purchases

Premium features are unlocked through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not show third-party ads.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your personal or location data to anyone.
  • Text recognition on receipts happens on your device (ML Kit); receipt images are not sent anywhere except your own Firebase Storage backup.
