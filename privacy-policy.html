Last updated: 20 October 2026

Overview

Last Letter: Merge & Mystery can be played without creating an account. Most of your game data never leaves your device. The only online feature is the optional global leaderboard, described below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (currencies, merge-board state, restoration progress, settings, and purchase records kept by the game) is stored only on your device. We do not operate our own servers that receive your gameplay data.

Global leaderboard (online feature)

If you open the in-game leaderboard ("Ranks"), the game creates an anonymous account using Firebase Authentication (a Google service) — no name, email address, or password is collected. The following data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and shown on the public leaderboard to other players:

  • a display name (by default "Barista #" followed by random digits; you can change it in the game),
  • an anonymous user ID generated by Firebase,
  • your best endless-mode level and total restoration stars,
  • the date and time your score was last updated.

This data is used only to operate the leaderboard. Firebase may also process technical data (such as device and app information) needed to provide authentication and prevent abuse, under Google's Firebase privacy information. If you never open the leaderboard, no leaderboard account is created and no score is uploaded.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (gem and star packs) processed by Google Play Billing. Purchases are real and are charged to your Google Play payment method. We do not see or store your payment card details; Google handles billing under its own privacy terms. The game records what was purchased on your device so your items can be delivered and restored.

Advertising

The game can show optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for in-game rewards). During the current testing phase, ads are served by Google AdMob using Google's official test ad units, so no real ad personalization occurs. When real ads are enabled, Google's advertising privacy practices will apply (see Google's Privacy Policy), and this policy will be updated first.

Analytics & crash reporting

The game does not run third-party analytics for advertising or profiling. If crash reporting is enabled in a future version to help fix technical problems, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The leaderboard display name is chosen by the player; players should not use their real name or any personal information as their display name.

Your choices & data deletion

You can stop all online data collection by not opening the leaderboard and by playing offline. To request deletion of your leaderboard entry and anonymous account data, contact us at alimughal198777@gmail.com and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
