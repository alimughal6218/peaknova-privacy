Privacy Policy — Charm Chain

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · App: Charm Chain (com.peaknova.charmchain) · Developer: PeakNova

Charm Chain is a cozy bead-chain puzzle game: aim and shoot pearls into a moving chain, match 3 or more of a kind to clear them, and build your dream bracelet. You can play it without creating an account.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Game progress — your best score, coin balance, owned and selected charms and velvet mats, and your sound setting.
  • Coin purchases — the coins you buy are added to your on-device balance so you can spend them on charms and mats.

This data lives only on your device. We operate no servers that receive your gameplay data, and the game has no account, no sign-in, and no leaderboard.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob — only optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch: one to continue a run after game over, and one for an extra Remover. There are no banner ads and no interstitial ads. You never have to watch an ad to play.

Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game offers three optional coin packs — 300 coins ($0.99), 1,000 coins ($2.99), and 2,500 coins ($4.99) — processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see or store your payment card details. Coins are consumable: they are added to the balance on your device and spent in the game on charms and mats. Because the balance lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes any unspent coins, and they cannot be restored on a new device.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising trackers of our own.
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no user accounts, and no way for players to share personal information with each other.
