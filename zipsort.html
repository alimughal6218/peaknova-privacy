Privacy Policy — Zip Sort

Developer: PeakNova (Ali Shahzad)
Contact: alimughal198777@gmail.com
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Overview

Zip Sort can be played without creating an account. Your game data never leaves your device.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked cases, coins, owned zipper charms, settings and sound preferences) is stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your gameplay data.

Leaderboard

The game includes a leaderboard screen, but no leaderboard server is connected in this version. It shows only your own best results stored on your device. No score, name or other data is uploaded anywhere.

In-app purchases

This version of Zip Sort offers no in-app purchases. The shop sells cosmetic zipper charms for coins you earn by playing.

Advertising

This version of Zip Sort shows no advertising of any kind.

Analytics & crash reporting

The game does not run third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
