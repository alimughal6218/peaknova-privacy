Privacy Policy — Mystic Wonders

Last updated: November 14, 2026 · App: Mystic Wonders (com.peaknova.mysticwonders) · Developer: PeakNova

Mystic Wonders is a cozy feed-the-animals puzzle game: serve the right food to the hungry jungle animals, earn coins and stars, and decorate your own oasis. You can play it without creating an account.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Game progress — your current level, stars earned, coin and gem balances, energy and its refill time, booster stock (Hint, Shuffle, Extra Time), total wins, daily quest progress, owned and equipped oasis decor, and your sound, music and vibration settings.

This data lives only on your device. We operate no servers that receive your gameplay data, and the game has no account, no sign-in, and no leaderboard. Coins and gems are earned by playing (feeding animals, winning levels, and completing daily quests); there is nothing to buy with real money.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for +30 energy, +30 seconds after losing a level, double coins after a win, or a free booster in the Shop), and an interstitial ad after every third win. You never have to watch an ad to play.

Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device). Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising trackers of our own.
  • We do not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It has no chat, no user accounts, and no way for players to share personal information with each other.
